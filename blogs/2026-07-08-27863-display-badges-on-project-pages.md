Hello, The Rising Star badge is only displayed for the first 6 months after a project reaches 100 monthly downloads. The badge is specifically intended to highlight new and emerging projects that have recently achieved that milestone. Once the 6-month period has passed, the badge expires and cannot be re-awarded or displayed permanently, as that would no longer reflect its purpose.
