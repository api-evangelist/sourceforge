<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Big News: SourceForge Just Got a Major Upgrade with Cool Demo Tools!</h2>



<p>Hey everyone! We&#8217;ve got some awesome news to share that&#8217;s going to make showcasing and exploring business software on SourceForge a whole lot cooler and easier. We&#8217;re now teamed up with some of the top interactive demo platforms out there:</p>

<li><strong>Supademo</strong></li>



<li><strong>Storylane</strong></li>



<li><strong>Reprise</strong></li>

<p>Yep, it&#8217;s a big deal, and here&#8217;s why.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Demos Just Got an Upgrade</h3>



<p>Let&#8217;s face it, seeing what software actually does and how it works is way more interesting than just reading about it. That&#8217;s why we&#8217;re super excited about making your software demos way more interactive and fun. With these tools, you can create demos that let people dive in and get their hands dirty with your software, right from the get-go.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Everything’s Better Together</h3>



<p>Integrating these platforms into SourceForge means you can now embed live, interactive demos right on your software listing page. Imagine being able to show off your software with a cool demo that people can actually play with without having to download anything or go somewhere else. It&#8217;s all about making life easier for your potential users and helping them see why your software rocks.</p>



<p>Here&#8217;s how it&#8217;ll look on the listing page:</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-15839" height="274" src="https://sourceforge.net/blog/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Screenshot-2024-04-02-at-4.24.18-PM-1024x274.png" width="1024" /></figure>



<p>Clicking on &#8220;View Demo&#8221; will open up the interactive demo right in the user&#8217;s browser window, without them leaving the page.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">A Toolbox Full of Goodies</h3>



<p>With a lineup like Supademo, Storylane, Reprise, and all the others, you&#8217;ve got a ton of options to choose from. Want to highlight specific features? Guide users through how things work? Show off different scenarios where your software shines? These platforms have got you covered, making it super simple to create demos that really show off what your software is all about.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Why This Rocks for You</h3>



<p>This isn&#8217;t just about making SourceForge look good (though that&#8217;s a nice bonus). It&#8217;s about giving you the tools to really connect with prospective buyers and show them what your software can do. It&#8217;s about making your software stand out and getting people excited to try it out. And let&#8217;s be honest, who doesn&#8217;t love playing around with a new tool before committing to it?</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">What’s Next?</h3>



<p>We&#8217;re pumped to see what you all come up with using these new demo tools. This is just the beginning, and we&#8217;re always on the lookout for ways to make SourceForge the best place to discover, share, and showcase software. So, dive in, start creating some amazing demos, and show the world what your software is made of!</p>
