<p>&#8212;-<br />
OSDN.net has been having extended service outages since it was recently acquired. Some users are reporting that OSDN has been down on and off for over a week. We&#8217;ve seen a big influx of new projects at SourceForge in the same time period.</p>
<p>Should OSDN remain inaccessible, we&#8217;d like to extend a warm invitation to all OSDN projects to host on SourceForge. As a reminder, SourceForge supports Git, SVN, and Mercurial. SourceForge also offers large binary downloads, project website hosting, and mailing lists, just like OSDN did.</p>
<p>You can create a project on SourceForge <a href="https://sourceforge.net/p/add_project">here</a>.</p>
